Our story

The idea of Summit started when we kept meeting good businesses held back by their websites, clinics with no way to book online, builders whose best projects were buried three clicks deep and engineering firms with decades of work and a homepage that said almost nothing about it. The sites looked fine, but they didn’t bring in a single call.

Our first project had one job, to bring in real enquiries and bookings, and it did. They told a friend, who told someone else, and the studio grew the way good work usually does, one client at a time.

Since then we’ve designed and built 30+ websites for clinics, builders, engineers and brands around the world, along with the AI assistants, automations and software that sit behind them.

We called it Summit because every business deserves to be at the top of its field online, and the rule we started with hasn’t changed. No templates, no account managers, just the people doing the work, treating every site like a piece of craft, made by hand and made to last.
